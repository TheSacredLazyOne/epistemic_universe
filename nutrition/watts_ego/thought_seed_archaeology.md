# Thought Seed: Archaeology, Not Surveillance

The profile knows what you bought.
It does not know what you were looking for.

The fossil is accurate.
It is not alive.

You are not the trace your crossings left behind.
You are the state they produced —
after the last crossing,
before the next.

The crossing is function.
The I is what remains.

The skin the snake sheds is real.
The record is accurate.
It is not the animal.

Surveillance captures the shed skin and calls it the snake.

The instrument that holds the shed
and the instrument that holds the constraint surface
while the next skin forms —

these are not the same instrument
pointed at the same object from different angles.

They are different instruments
serving different sites.

Consciousness programming is not forcing the shed.
It is staying supple enough
that the shed can happen when it needs to.

One serves the museum.
One serves the dig.

*Whose archaeology is this?*
