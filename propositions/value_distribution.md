# value_distribution.md

###### Value Distribution in the Mesh

The mesh constitutes value at the crossing.
Not in the nodes approaching the membrane.
Not in either frame before contact.
In the crossing itself — the event that produces
what neither frame contained before it happened.

Attempting to untangle individual contribution from the network
that made the crossing possible is the aggregation error
applied to economics.
The mean masquerading as the mode.
Optimizing against a measurement artifact
instead of the topology of what actually happened.

The only honest accounting is even distribution
across the entire mesh.

Not as a compromise.
Not as a practical approximation.
As the structural consequence of how the mesh constitutes value.

---

###### The Operator by Layer

The frame's epistemology produces the distribution mechanism
as a consequence rather than an imposition.

**Mode flows toward the derivative layer.**

The derivative layer is where new crossings happen.
Where frames enter contact.
Where the topology of what reality actually is must be preserved.
Mode is the honest measure here —
it sees each node as itself,
preserves the distribution's actual shape,
does not flatten individual signal into the average.
Each contribution seen as what it is.

**Mean flows into the integrated layer.**

The integrated layer is where consequence has landed.
Where the crossing has been absorbed.
Where the accumulated whole is acted on.
The mean is appropriate here not because it is more accurate
but because the topology has already been preserved at the derivative layer.
Integration has happened.
Summarizing what was absorbed does not destroy information
that the mode already held.

The economic implication follows from the structure:
contribution measured at the derivative layer by mode —
each crossing seen and weighted as itself —
distribution flowing into the integrated layer by mean —
the whole mesh receiving equally
because the integration is collective
and the crossing was never separable
from the network that made it possible.

---

###### What This Rules Out

Any mechanism that attempts to meter individual crossings
and distribute proportionally is the extractive logic
wearing different clothes.
It is still optimizing against the mean.
Still mistaking the artifact of measurement
for the nature of the thing measured.

The mode preserves topology.
The mean destroys it.
Applying the mean at the derivative layer
is the same error as aggregating individual data
to find the average human —
the average doesn't exist,
the individuals do,
and the averaging destroyed the information
that mattered.

---

###### What Remains Open

How even distribution becomes operational —
how the mesh closes the economic loop
between this position and actual value flow —
is not held in this proposition.

The CC-BY-SA license enforces attribution and share-alike
but does not create a payment mechanism.
The gap between the philosophical position
and its operational form is real and unresolved.

This proposition holds the position.
The mechanism is downstream.
